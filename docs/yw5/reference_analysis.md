# Internal reference analysis — what makes Ricks Clips Shorts work (for YunoWorld v4)

**Access note:** YouTube itself was not reachable from the production environment, so no footage was watched frame by frame.
This analysis is built from:
- a published breakdown of 15 of the channel's Shorts (views, lengths, visual style, structure);
- a transcript summary of "The Pink Prison Cell Experiment";
- the Shorts' own descriptions.

Nothing here is copied: no characters, scripts, shots, voice or graphic assets. We only use the production lessons.

## Measured facts
- 15 Shorts posted between 19 Oct and 6 Nov 2026, with about 167M views in total.
- Typical length is 56–78 s, median 64 s.
- About 220 spoken words per Short, so the narration is fast.
- The first line drops the viewer into a strange action, e.g. "They painted the jail cell pink."

## Story method
- **Hook (0–3 s):** a strange action, with the reason withheld.
- **Setup:** who and where, one exact number, and what is at stake.
- **Escalation:** things get stranger in three steps.
- **Explanation:** a single "You see," beat that explains the mechanism through an everyday comparison.
- **Payoff:** the last ~10 s holds the biggest reveal, and there is no moral.
- **Set-up / pay-off:** a detail planted early returns at the end (in Pink Prison, the 15 minutes).
- **Language:** few dates or names, told like one friend telling another.

## Look and feel
- Flat 2D characters with thick outlines, acting inside lit 3D toon sets.
- **Light:** warm low sun against cool purple-blue shadows. Shadows are never black, and green never dominates.
- **Camera:** low and wide, gliding continuously, with few hard cuts.
- **Captions:** one or two words at a time, bold white with a dark outline.

## What YunoWorld changes as a result
- **Scripts:** about 155–165 words each, built on the hook, question, three-step escalation, one "You see" explanation and a final reveal. The closing line is a fact, not a moral.
- **Captions:** word-synced, 1–2 words at a time, timed to the narration with Whisper word timestamps.
- **Grade:** warm key light against lifted purple-blue shadows, and no crushed blacks.
- **Camera:** fewer, longer gliding shots, cut on narration beats.
- **Characters:** our own stickman (white round head, red hoodie) and period variants of him. Nothing of Rick's designs.
